Cookies Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Virtuole uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure the secure and efficient operation of our platform. This policy outlines how and why we use these technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They allow the server to remember your session states and preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: We use secure, encrypted session cookies to authenticate your identity on the Virtuole Gateway and keep you securely logged into your dashboard (Intern, Mentor, Ambassador, or Admin).
  • Security Measures: We utilize cookies to prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and bypass reverse proxy loops.
  • Theme Preferences: We store a small local token to remember if you prefer Light Mode or Dark Mode.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Since we utilize secure payment gateways (like PhonePe), third-party cookies may be temporarily placed during the payment verification process to ensure transaction integrity.

4. Managing Your Cookies

You have the right to block cookies via your browser settings. However, please be aware that disabling Strictly Necessary cookies will result in the Virtuole platform failing to authenticate your login, and you will not be able to access your dashboard.
